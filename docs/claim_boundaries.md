# Claim boundaries

1. The experiment characterizes temporal dynamic class imbalance and minority-sensitive online performance; it does not claim a new classifier.
2. Observed prevalence/lifecycle change is not itself ground-truth concept drift.
3. Formal support-recall and minority-state coefficients are associations, not causal effects.
4. MOOB is not claimed to be best for every class, time period, or metric; class-specific reversals and benign-FPR trade-offs remain.
5. MUOB's benign-only behavior is specific to the evaluated global-minimum update rule under the closed-world episodic taxonomy; it is not evidence that undersampling is universally invalid.
6. Immediate labels are an experimental assumption.
7. The 50-observation support floor is an operational evidence-qualification rule, not a universal recall-stability guarantee.
